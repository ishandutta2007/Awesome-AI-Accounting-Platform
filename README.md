This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Accounting**. These systems use AI to categorize transactions, code invoices, reconcile banks, accelerate month-end close, and reduce manual bookkeeping—for startups, SMBs, and accounting teams.



**Examples** include Numeric, Rillet, Puzzle, Truewind, Campfire, Booke AI, Docyt, Vic.ai, Digits, and Osfin AI (the category leaders).

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

  AI-oriented close and accounting operations platform focused on month-end workflows, reconciliations, and controller productivity.



- **[Rillet, Puzzle, Campfire](https://www.rillet.com/)**  

  Modern AI-native or AI-assisted accounting platforms for startups—automated bookkeeping, real-time ledgers, and streamlined financial operations.



- **[Truewind, Booke AI, Docyt, Vic.ai](https://www.truewind.ai/)**  

  AI bookkeeping and AP/AR automation platforms that categorize transactions, process invoices, and reduce manual data entry for firms and businesses.



- **[Digits, Osfin AI](https://digits.com/)**  

  AI-powered accounting and finance tools aimed at visibility, automation, and intelligent insights over the books.



- **[Other commercial AI accounting platforms](https://www.numeric.io/)**  

  Additional solutions for autonomous bookkeeping, audit support, and finance team automation.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Full open-source ERP with strong accounting, invoicing, multi-company, and inventory modules—widely used as a self-hosted financial system foundation; AI features can be layered via connectors or custom agents.



- **[ERPClaw](https://github.com/avansaber/erpclaw)**  

  Open-source AI-native ERP experiment—accounting, inventory, sales, and purchasing operated in plain language, with double-entry books and self-hosted control (GPL).



- **[LedgerMind (ERPNext connector)](https://github.com/WisdmLabs/ledgermind)**  

  Open ERPNext plugin connecting to AI-powered finance automation (bank reconciliation, AP coding, close assistance) with human-in-the-loop approval patterns.



- **[Firefly III](https://github.com/firefly-iii/firefly-iii)**  

  Popular open-source personal and small-business finance manager—double-entry, budgets, rules; privacy-focused (not full corporate AI accounting, but a solid ledger core).



- **[Akaunting, Invoice Ninja & open invoicing](https://github.com/akaunting/akaunting)**  

  Open-source accounting and invoicing applications for SMBs that can be extended with automation scripts or AI categorization services.



- **[Beancount, Ledger & plain-text accounting](https://github.com/beancount/beancount)**  

  Plain-text double-entry systems favored by technical users; often scripted with custom AI categorization pipelines.



- **[AI-native accounting research / PoC projects](https://github.com/search?q=AI+accounting+OR+AI+bookkeeping+open+source)**  

  Early open initiatives exploring LLM-driven journal entries, categorization, and close workflows on top of proper ledgers.



- **[OCR & document AI open tools](https://github.com/search?q=invoice+OCR+OR+receipt+parser+open+source)**  

  Open invoice/receipt parsers that feed structured data into open or commercial ledgers.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Full business accounting**: ERPNext as the primary open ERP/accounting backbone.

- **AI-native experiments**: ERPClaw and similar projects for chat-operated books.

- **Personal / small ledger**: Firefly III and plain-text accounting for simpler needs.

- **Composable stacks**: OCR → categorization model → ERPNext/Firefly journal API for DIY AI bookkeeping.

- Commercial platforms still lead in polished close workflows, bank connectivity, and firm-ready automation.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

**ERPNext** is the strongest open accounting foundation; **Firefly III** and **Akaunting** suit lighter needs.  

Layer open OCR and LLM categorization (or connectors like LedgerMind) for AI-assisted workflows.  

Commercial AI accounting platforms (Numeric, Rillet, Puzzle, Docyt, Vic.ai, etc.) deliver end-to-end automation and support.  

Many startups use commercial AI bookkeeping for speed and open ERPNext for operational control. Fully open stacks work when you can own bank feeds, categorization quality, and audit processes.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Accounting systems affect tax, audit, and legal compliance. AI categorization and journal suggestions must be reviewed by qualified humans. Incorrect books can create regulatory and financial risk.

- Open-source tools offer data ownership but require correct setup, backups, and professional oversight. Commercial platforms shift operational burden to the vendor. Neither replaces a qualified accountant or auditor where required.
